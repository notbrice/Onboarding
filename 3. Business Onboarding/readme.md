# Business Onboarding

The Business department specializes in strategically promoting our projects and ensuring our achievements are recognised. It's not just about making things look good; it's about building a professional brand that resonates with students, sponsors, and industry partners. Business also invloves directly connecting with industry players, to secure partenerships and sponsorships.  

Business members could be involved in graphic design for marketing material, website development, social media, creating marketing strategies, designing merchandise, writing newsletters, researching potential industry parteners, contacting said industy players, negotiating contracts, applying for government grants, etc. Business members should be familiar with PAST's brand guidelines and understand visual elements and principles. Not only that, they should keep up to date with the projects and progress happening within the team to be able to communicate it well.

If you would like to be a Business member there should be evidence of Business project work in your logbook.
